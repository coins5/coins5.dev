# Strategio: AI-Powered Business Intelligence

Strategio is a platform designed to make business intelligence accessible. As the ex-CTO, I designed and developed the entire system from the ground up.

## The Challenge

Startups in the BI space face high operational costs and complex user interfaces. The goal was to build a system where founders and managers could classify products and get automated business reports easily on both web and mobile devices.

## The Solution

We implemented an event-driven modular architecture leveraging OpenAI and PostgreSQL vectors. This resulted in:
1. Fast classification of millions of data points.
2. Low latencies and extremely cost-efficient querying.
3. Clean user flows.
